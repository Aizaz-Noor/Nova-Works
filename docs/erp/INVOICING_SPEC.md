# Client invoicing slice

Flow: accepted immutable sales order -> one original draft invoice -> issue with company-controlled INV number -> record manual payments -> reverse erroneous payment records or fully credit an unpaid invoice -> replacement draft preserving original history. This is an operational invoice ledger, not a payment processor, accounting certification or tax engine.

OWNER/ADMIN only for all invoice/credit/PDF routes. Other company roles403; all data strictly company scoped with composite FKs/forcedRLS. Invoice line/amount/customer/currency/terms snapshots copy the accepted sales order exactly. Draft edits limited to issuerName/issuerAddress/customerAddress/paymentInstructions/dueOn. Issue fixes original payload, issuedOn=currentUTCdate, dueOn>=issuedOn if supplied, and immutable INV-000001 sequential number unique per company. Counters (INVOICE/CREDIT) lock transactionally and roll back with failed writes; no yearly reset or editable number. Drafts have no number. Original creation serializes the order natural key with an advisory transaction lock on PostgreSQL (SQLite write transactions serialize it) and safely reopens generation1; replacements require fullcredit of source and one-child uniqueness. One active full invoice per order lineage; no partial/order-splitting or time billing yet.

Invoice status DRAFT/ISSUED only; settlement is derived from append-only payment and credit rows. Money canonical integer minor-unit strings0..9007199254740991. Invoice items/totals copied, not recalculated in browser. Different currencies are never aggregated. Due balance=originaltotal-activepayments-fullcredit; no negative balance/overpayment. Payment {version,requestId,amountMinor,currency,paidOn,reference}; amountpositive,currencymatchesinvoice,paidOn<=currentUTCdate,reference required and unique for originalpayments on the invoice. Same original payload/requestkey replays after permission checks; changed payload409. Every ledger mutation locks invoice and increments its version. Payment reversal {version,reason} appends equal opposite ledger record, at most once per original; same reason replays. It records a correction and moves no money.

Full credit {version,reason} only on issued invoice with positive total and zero activepayments/no prior credit; stores original subtotal/tax/total/currency and fixed CN number. Same reason replays. Partial credits/refunds are deferred. Replacement {version} only afterfullcredit; generation+1 copies the immutable order and old draft addresses/instructions but clears dueOn; returns same child on retries. Original invoice/credit remain visible. Fully credited invoices cannot receive new payments. Payment reversal does not erase its original record or reference.

Routes /api/v1/client-invoices GET paged q/status, POST{salesOrderId}; /:id GET/PATCH; /:id/{issue,payments,credit,replacement} POST; /:id/payments/:paymentId/reverse POST; /:id/pdf GET. /credit-notes/:id/pdf GET. Invoice details include items/payments/credits and derived paidMinor/creditedMinor/balanceMinor/settlement. Mutations strict allowed fields/NUL/type/version validation and audit. Append-only payments/credits/items; runtime counters SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE, invoices SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE, documenthistory SELECT/INSERT. Database guards freeze invoice snapshot/identity, issued header, and all existing item/payment/credit rows.

Migration008 additive;002-007 checksums unchanged. PDFKit pinned with scriptsdisabled, standalone server buffer generation, no filesystem/cloud/browser process dependency. PDFs are readable exports of issued snapshots, not bank/payment evidence. Initial built-in PDF font export supports printableASCII plus linebreaks; unsupported text returns clear422 while savedUnicode records remain readable online. English-only export is an explicit initial limitation, not silent text substitution. Render/extract/check actual invoice and credit PDFs including multipage longterms before acceptance.
