# NovaWorks workflow tutorial

This silent, captioned video shows an edited end-to-end workflow using fictional data in the isolated local demo. It is not continuous real-time footage and does not show every click. Credentials are not shown; no outside customer was contacted and no money moved.

1. Create and qualify a lead, then convert it into linked customer, contact and deal records.
2. Prepare a quote, record acceptance and convert it into a sales order.
3. Mark the deal Won and create its linked delivery project with a manager and deadline.
4. Assign a task, update its status and submit time.
5. Have a different account review and approve the time entry.
6. Create an invoice from the order and record fictional payment evidence.
7. Review the reports for the resulting activity.

The video demonstrates a fixed-price workflow. It does not demonstrate live AI generation, email delivery, payment processing or the separate time-based invoicing flow. The workflow states shown by the interface are distinct: task completion, time approval, invoice issue and payment evidence.
